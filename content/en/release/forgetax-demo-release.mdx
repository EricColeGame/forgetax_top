### What a Demo Is — and What It Isn't

A demo is a curated slice, not a beta. It is polished to a specific standard, cut to a specific length, and designed to end at a moment that leaves you wanting more. Understanding that distinction keeps your expectations calibrated and makes your feedback far more useful to the team.

- **It is:** a representative vertical slice of the core loop.
- **It is not:** the full game with a lock on it.
- **It is:** usually feature-complete for the systems it shows.
- **It is not:** a promise that every system in the demo ships unchanged.

## A Practical Setup Checklist for Your First Session

Demos are often less optimized than the final build, so a little setup goes a long way. Use this checklist as a quick pass before your first run.

| Setting | Recommended Starting Point | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Resolution | Native display resolution | Drop to 1080p if frame rate suffers |
| Frame rate cap | Match your monitor's refresh rate | Reduces coil whine and heat |
| V-Sync | Off initially, on if you see tearing | Adds slight input latency when enabled |
| Field of view | Nudge up one step from default | Helps with motion comfort |
| Subtitles | On | Demos often mix audio poorly in early builds |
| Motion blur | Off | Makes it easier to judge visual clarity |
| Keybinds | Rebind before you judge the controls | Defaults are rarely final |

Play for at least thirty minutes before forming an opinion. Opening sequences are the most heavily scripted part of any demo and the least representative of the moment-to-moment loop.

## How to Give Feedback That Actually Shapes the Game

Feedback is where players have real influence, and the forgetax demo release is designed to collect it. The difference between useful and useless feedback comes down to specificity. "It felt bad" tells a developer nothing. "The dodge input registered roughly half a second after I pressed the button on a wired controller" gives them something they can reproduce and fix.

| Feedback Type | Weak Version | Strong Version |
|---|---|---|
| Performance | "It lags" | "Frame drops to the low 40s in the crowded market area on a mid-range GPU" |
| Controls | "Controls are weird" | "Camera sensitivity feels too high on default; aim assist snaps past targets" |
| Difficulty | "Too hard" | "The second encounter has no safe window to heal between waves" |
| UI | "Confusing menu" | "I could not find the inventory tab without opening the map first" |
| Bugs | "It crashed" | "Crash on load after alt-tabbing; occurred twice in five attempts" |

Always include your platform, hardware, and build identifier if one is shown on the main menu. Reproducibility is the currency of bug reports.

### Where to Send It
