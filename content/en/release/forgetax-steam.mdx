## How to Wishlist and Track Forgetax on Steam

The wishlist button is the single highest-value click you can make before launch. It costs nothing and it converts into a notification the instant the game becomes purchasable.

**Step-by-step:**

1. Sign in to your Steam account in a browser or the desktop client — wishlists tied to a guest session do not sync.
2. Search for the game on the store and open its product page.
3. Click **Add to your wishlist**. Confirm it appears under your account's wishlist tab.
4. On the same page, click **Follow** to receive developer posts and patch notes.
5. Scroll to the community hub and join it if you want discussion threads in your feed.
6. Verify your notification settings so Steam emails and client pop-ups are not muted.

That last step is where most people lose their advantage. Steam will happily send you a launch alert that you never see because desktop notifications are disabled or the email landed in a promotions folder.

## Sorting Real Release News From Noise

Release-date chatter is where misinformation spreads fastest. Someone sees a placeholder date in a database, posts a screenshot, and within a day it is treated as confirmed. Here is how to grade what you read.

| Signal | Reliability | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Developer's own Steam news post | Very high | The studio controls the message |
| Steam store page "coming soon" date | High | Set by the publisher, still subject to change |
| Verified publisher social account | High | Official channel, timestamped |
| Steam database backend changes | Medium | Real data, but often incomplete or temporary |
| Community reports and forum leaks | Low | Unverified, frequently misread |
| "Insider" videos with no source | Very low | Engagement-driven, rarely accountable |

A useful rule: if a date cannot be traced back to the developer or the store page itself, treat it as a rumor and plan accordingly. Dates slip. Delays are normal, especially for small teams polishing a build before certification.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I get notified about the Forgetax Steam release?**

Wishlist the game while signed in to your account, then confirm that Steam client notifications and account emails are not muted. The wishlist alert is the most reliable notification you will get, and it fires the moment the store page becomes purchasable.

**Is the release date on the store page final?**

Treat store dates as strong intentions rather than guarantees. Publishers set them, publishers can move them, and delays are common for independent teams. Only a developer's own announcement should be treated as close to certain.

**Should I buy at launch or wait?**

Buying at launch supports the developer and gets you into the discussion early. Waiting gives you patch stability and clearer reviews. If you are uncertain, use the refund window to test the game rather than guessing.

**Where can I find accurate system requirements?**

The Steam store page lists minimum and recommended specifications under the system requirements section. Always compare against your actual hardware, not the machine you plan to upgrade to.

**What if the store page looks unfinished?**

Some listings go live before all media and details are added. Bookmark the page, wishlist it anyway, and check back — an incomplete listing usually fills in closer to launch.
