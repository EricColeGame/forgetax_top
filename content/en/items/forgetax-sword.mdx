## How Players Get the Forgetax Sword

There is rarely one "correct" route to a rare sword. Most players pick between three paths: farming the drop, completing the associated quest or crafting chain, or buying it from another player. Each has a different mix of time, luck, and risk.

### Route 1: Boss and Dungeon Farming

Farming is the classic approach and the one most guides default to. You repeat a specific encounter until the drop table finally cooperates. The upside is that you keep everything you earn along the way — materials, currency, and practice. The downside is variance: some players get lucky on their first handful of runs, while others grind far longer than expected.

- Always confirm you are fighting the correct difficulty tier, since drop tables often differ.
- Bring a group if the encounter supports it; more clears per hour usually beats solo efficiency.
- Track your runs so you can judge whether your route is actually productive.

### Route 2: Crafting, Quests, and Events

If the Forgetax Sword is craftable or quest-locked, this route trades luck for structure. You know exactly what you need, and progress is visible. The catch is that materials are often gated behind the same bosses you would have farmed anyway, plus limited-time events that can disappear for months.

### Route 3: Trading

Trading is the fastest route and the riskiest. Player experience suggests the market for a rare sword like this is thin, which means prices swing hard and bad actors target buyers who seem rushed.

| Route | Time Cost | RNG Dependence | Risk Level | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Boss farming | High | Very high | Low | Patient solo players |
| Crafting / quest chain | Medium–high | Low | Low | Players who prefer guaranteed progress |
| Limited-time event | Low–medium | Medium | Medium (missable) | Active players during the event window |
| Player trading | Low | None | High (scams) | Players with spare currency |

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

- **Buying before checking your stats.** A rare sword that scales with stats you never leveled is an expensive decoration.
- **Ignoring the upgrade path.** Factor in the cost of enhancing, repairing, and socketing before you commit.
- **Farming the wrong difficulty tier.** Drop tables often differ between tiers, and the "harder" version is not always the better farm.
- **Trading without a middleman.** Use trusted trade systems or verified middlemen whenever the platform offers them.
- **Skipping the event window.** Limited-time sources can vanish for months; check the schedule early.
- **Overpaying during hype.** Newly buffed or newly discovered items spike in price. Wait a week if you can.

## FAQ

**Is the Forgetax Sword worth farming?**
For most mid-to-late game melee players, yes — provided it scales with stats you have already invested in. If you are early in progression or running a caster build, your time is better spent elsewhere first.

**Can I get the Forgetax Sword without trading?**
Usually yes. Boss farming, quest chains, and limited-time events are the primary sources. Trading exists mainly as a shortcut for players who would rather spend currency than hours.

**What should I pair with the Forgetax Sword?**
Prioritize crit chance, attack speed, and survivability. Anything that increases your melee uptime — lifesteal, defense, or mobility — will outperform a small flat damage boost.

**How do I avoid getting scammed when trading for the Forgetax Sword?**
Use in-game trade windows rather than dropping items, never trust "trust trades," and confirm the item's exact name and stats before accepting. Player experience shows that rushed buyers are the most common targets.
